One calm upper-cabinet composition

The 1005 mm upper group contains a 600 mm dish-drainer cabinet beside a separate 405 mm dry-food cupboard, with full-height hinged doors and no horizontal front split. High storage stays behind the same doors. Drying and food storage are separated by complete cabinet sides. Interiors are shown separately.

Half-open prep counter

Cabinet1372 ×762 mm; stone1382 ×782 mm. Free-end corners: cabinet R50 / stone R60,10 mm projection on three exposed edges and flush at fridge. Drawer fronts stop beside the curved return; confirm the detail with Jason. Keep the quoted 1372 mm cabinet width: 600 mm of serving drawers plus a 772 mm open bay. The open bay has a 406 mm upper compartment, an 18 mm divider and a 320 mm lower rice-cooker compartment. A 450 mm-high oven will not fit the split.

Oven position before appliance purchase

The open bay has a dashed oven position marker, not an approved appliance. Select a model that permits under-counter cabinet installation before fixing the shelf, ventilation and infill. Keeping a freestanding oven instead means using the separate countertop alternative.

Daily storage and service access

Serving: cutlery, plates / bowls and containers together in the 600 mm prep drawers. Cooking: oil / sauces beside the hob, pots / steamer below. Washing: drainer above the sink; water equipment and cleaning products below. Food: sealed grains and snacks in the separate upper cupboard. Electric rice cooker: lower open bay, storage only.

Before Jason makes the shop drawing

Five items to settle.

  1. Measure the wet wall: source plan 2065 mm versus quoted 7 ft / 2134 mm. This design uses 2065; it does not stretch the wall.
  2. Price the internal change: half-open base, additional drawer bank and revised cabinet modules need Jason’s confirmation. The optional 1900 × 700 study changes the quoted 1372 × 762 footprint; it is not the default design.
  3. Select the appliances: actual fridge ventilation, hob cut-out / supply and oven installation type. No oven has been selected. The base-bay placeholder needs an under-counter approved appliance; the Toshiba is shown only in the countertop alternative.
  4. Confirm sink services: included 600 mm sink and tap, bowl depth, drain/trap, LG installation space and accessible dry-side sockets. The purifier faucet may need an additional 28 mm hole beyond the quoted hob and sink cut-outs.
  5. Finish the hood and window details: supplier-approved recirculation kit, filter access, return grille; remove the kitchen-to-yard louvre and form a yard-facing niche, closed on the kitchen side, matching the uppers. Measure the opening and available depth.
Planning references and measured limits

NKBA kitchen planning guidelines: reference work aisles are 1067 mm for one cook and 1219 mm for multiple cooks. These are planning recommendations, not a claim of Malaysian statutory compliance. This galley is designed principally for one person working at a time. Its short wet run cannot meet every recommended sink / hob landing allowance; the opposite counter carries the main preparation task.

Blum workflow / storage zones: consumables, tableware, cleaning, preparation and cooking. Frequently used contents go in reachable drawers; appliance and cookware storage stay close to their tasks.

VATTI V997: 896 W × 360 D mm; lower body 421 mm H, total body/chimney envelope 908 mm H; chimney 420 mm W; 910 mm opening; 300–380 mm hob-to-hood-bottom reference. A 900 mm counter and 300 mm gap are drawn here. The front outline is corrected from the manufacturer’s dimension sheet, page 2: only the lower 421 mm body is drawn full width; the narrower chimney is concealed behind removable cabinet fronts. Confirm the supplier’s exact recirculation fittings before fabrication; no external coring is added.

Häfele Malaysia dish-drainer reference: 600 / 900 mm cabinet formats; rack depth 275 mm. This verifies a market format, not a selected purchase. Jason must confirm the included rack, removable tray and brackets.

LG ATOM-U WU525BS: 170 W × 410 D × 420 H main unit; manufacturer asks for about 100 mm extra installation space and a 28 mm faucet hole, with larger fixing / ring clearance. The sketch reserves space, not permission to drill.

Toshiba MW3-SAC24SF(BK): 469 W × 415 D × 280 H, microwave + convection + grill + air fry. Its manual shows 300 mm above and 200 mm beside adjacent walls, with countertop placement over 850 mm. Its manual also specifies freestanding use, no untested cabinet installation, and a minimum installation height of 850 mm. It is not approved for the drawn base bay. The alternative below-counter position needs another model and its installation drawing.

Plan geometry is registered to Original Plan Type A: wet run x296–457 = 2065 mm; opposing-counter back line y741 to wet wall y931 = 2450 mm. Door hinge / radius are traced at x166, y875, radius84 px. A 50 mm extra sweep allowance is used in the entry-door check. Cabinet heights, 2850 mm ceiling, 900 mm worktops, 18 mm board and appliance envelopes remain proposed / unmeasured.
