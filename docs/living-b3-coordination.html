Design conclusion

Bedroom 3 · plan and height must agree

The full-height door is the issue.

The current model has four approximately 457 mm main-door bays plus four aligned hinged loft doors. The upper doors extend from 2260 to 2820 mm and their sweep shares the potential fan-blade height.

Proposed cabinet detail: split the main doors below the lowest blade envelope, with an additional site-set allowance. Use four aligned hinged loft doors above, as selected by the owner. Upper doors still swing in the blade-height band and must be checked against the actual fan.

2260 mm main-door top and 2550 mm blade height in this illustration are assumptions. Measure the real ceiling, complete fan drop, blade travel, door top, handles and hinges before making this detail.

Source registration and calculations

Living coordinates: left finished wall and window-side finished face are the datum. Vertical scale uses the adjacent B2 2517 mm reference across 270 electrical pixels. Living is open to Dining; the bottom of the drawing is a crop, not a wall.

Bedroom 3 coordinates: left finished wall and wardrobe-side/north wall are the datum. Room 2865 × 2450 mm. Electrical inside faces: x832–1144, y720–995. Architect inside faces: x534–755, y535–725. The cabinet is 1829 × 600 mm, ending at the right wall; four nominal leaves of 457.25 mm.

Clearances use distances to segments, circles, rectangles and the entire 0–90° swept door sector. Track lamp reach toward the fan is conservatively allowed as 75 mm; actual fitting tilt and length must replace that assumption. Door thickness/handles use an additional 25 mm plan allowance. The dashed envelope is a screening offset around the blade disk, not another physical component.

Beam shadows and perceived flicker are not established by a plan projection. Fan blades blocking a wide light distribution may create moving shadows even when lamp bodies clear; test the selected fittings at their real heights.
