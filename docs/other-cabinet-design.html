Material, hardware and measurements to settle
  • Eucalli E0 plywood is the owner-confirmed material direction. Drawings assume 18 mm carcass / fronts and a 9 mm back; these thicknesses and surface codes are proposed specifications, not additional inclusions proven by the quotation.
  • Use full-extension soft-close drawer runners sized and rated for the actual contents. Internal drawers must clear the selected door hinges and mirror. Bedroom 3 drawers are external, below its left hanging doors; runner allowances remain subject to hardware.
  • Bedroom 2 width is the latest 1440 mm design, replacing the older 1829 mm quote / niche. Bedroom 3 remains 1829 mm. Confirm revised quantities, drawer sets, mirror, loft-door hinges and any price changes with Jason.
  • 2850 mm room height is a model assumption. Wardrobe bodies are drawn to 2820 mm plus a 30 mm site-cut top scribe. Shoe cabinet body is 2700 mm, floated 120 mm, also finishing at 2820 mm. Measure completed floor, ceiling, skirting and wall plumb before fabrication.
  • Bedroom 3 uses four hinged main doors and four aligned hinged loft doors. The upper-door swing must be checked against the real fan; splitting the doors alone does not establish clearance. Actual blade height, sag, fixing and door clearances govern.
  • Television width / height are a 65-inch context reference only. Verify the purchased TV, Wireless One Connect box, power lead, outlet access, ventilation and wireless signal before fixing openings. Wireless video does not remove the TV’s mains-power cable.
  • Floating shoe storage needs a fixing design for the actual wall and loaded weight. Confirm the entrance / grille swings and exit path on site. No common-corridor location or new wall-chasing is approved here.
