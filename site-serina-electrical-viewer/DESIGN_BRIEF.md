# Serina web app design brief

This file keeps the user's accepted constraints together so later edits do not silently replace earlier decisions. The 3D viewer is a planning visual, not an as-built or construction drawing. Structural walls/windows are traced from the developer's Type A plan and checked against actual-unit photos; developer electrical points are diagram translations that still need site measurements. Blender furniture is not a design source.

## Unit and furniture

- Existing ceiling/slab height: approximately 2.85 m. Common-area flooring: glossy 600 × 600 mm warm-grey tile, calibrated against the actual-unit tile close-up and full-room photos. Bedrooms have pale neutral grey-oak-look flooring, checked against B2 and master photos. The photos vary markedly in exposure and white balance, so neither texture is an exact product match.
- Wall palette: Jotun 1928 Summer Snow. The model base wall colour is anchored to the visible official Jotun on-screen swatch, RGB 229/226/216 (#E5E2D8), rather than its written colour description. Approximate lighting is 3500 K. The model is white-balanced to avoid a false orange wall at night; a real painted sample under the intended fixtures remains the deciding check.
- Living sofa: King Living 1978 in Fortitude Linen, not the old Blender sofa. TV stays on the TV wall; a black floor-level magazine rack replaces a TV cabinet.
- Dining: 1600 mm travertine-look table with black sculpted base, two light upholstered chairs with black frames, and a light upholstered bench against the Bath 2 wall. Chairs tuck under the table.
- Kitchen: dark graphite wet-run base cabinetry, light grey upper and fridge doors, grey modern ash-look fridge cheek, and a 1370 mm open prep-counter base in **exactly the same light-grey finish as the upper/fridge doors**. This reduces the number of cabinet colours while leaving the dark wet run as the anchor. The prep counter has no upper cabinetry. The counter surface is light stone-look. Final joinery sizes follow site measurement and Ace's final shop drawings.
- B2 and B3 wardrobes: pale matte mineral-grey fronts, slim black pulls and a small door-side open niche lined in restrained grey-ash grain. The lighter fronts keep the small rooms from becoming a dark block beside the light bedroom floor. B2 retains three full-height doors plus its separate niche bay; B3 retains three full-height doors plus a fourth, partly open door-side bay. Confirm the final laminate with a physical sample under both daylight and 3500 K light.
- Entry shoe cabinet: beside the inner main door (the outer opening is the grill). Owner specified a floating 2700 mm cabinet. The model treats that as **2700 mm cabinet-body height**, beginning 120 mm above finished floor and ending at 2820 mm AFFL, with a nominal 30 mm site-cut scribe to the approximate 2850 mm slab. The model width 820 mm and depth 380 mm are design targets only. Four short, matte light-mineral-grey handleless doors give practical leaf sizes. Check the actual wall length, main-door sweep, entry clear width, wall anchor capacity and existing entry socket before Ace issues shop drawings; do not bury that outlet.

## Scheme A — existing-point / local pelmets

- Living: full-wall window pelmet and slim sofa-wall uplight box; original ceiling is otherwise kept. Globe pendant is visually shown at the second living light point with a surface hook; original fan point remains indicated.
- Master: full-width double curtains and window pelmet light; no fan. The central surface fixture stays available as a separate circuit, while two small shielded bedside lamps provide phone/bedtime light without looking at a ceiling diffuser.
- B2: original ceiling, central slim surface light and small plug-in circulator in the open wardrobe niche.
- B3: original ceiling, central slim surface light; compact ceiling-mounted wall fan at the original Bath-2-side point, subject to structural anchor and electrical check.

## Scheme B — 100 mm flat ceiling

- Common area, hall, master and B2 only: finished flat ceiling approximately 2.75 m. B3 keeps Scheme A's original ceiling and fixtures. Bath 1, Bath 2, yard and AC ledge are not included in the new plaster plane.
- The living/master/B2 window edges use open recessed linear LED slots, not dropped curtain pelmets. In Scheme B the living sofa-wall L-box is removed. LED light must read as a continuous diffuse wash, not isolated spots, and the emitters are hidden from the standing view. Drivers need accessible service locations.
- Living: 48-inch ceiling fan centred on the developer fan point, with its visible mounting lowered with the new ceiling while still anchored to structural slab. No downlight is over the sofa or chaise. One recessed, deep-cup anti-glare downlight sits near the sofa wall **in front of** the sofa, and two serve the TV-side circulation. Check fan blade clearance and moving shadows on site.
- Recessed lights are represented as 120 mm cut-outs with a matte black baffle and diffuser about 56 mm above the finished plane. This is a visual/specification target, not a selected product; confirm real cut-out, recess depth, heat clearance and beam angle with the supplier before the electrician cuts plaster.
- Dining: slim linear up/down pendant centred above the 1600 mm table. Its wiring position is proposed under the flat ceiling, not the developer outlet coordinate.
- Entry: one recessed deep-cup anti-glare downlight. Kitchen wet run: two recessed deep-cup anti-glare downlights shifted toward the working edge of the counter and aimed at its surface, plus a separate continuous under-upper-cabinet task strip. A third lower-power deep-cup downlight serves the fridge cabinet front. Open prep counter: three small down-facing pendants on one circuit, spaced across the 1370 mm top, with their shades above the standing sightline and clear of the aisle.
- Hall: two matching recessed deep-cup anti-glare downlights along the passage. The hall and common-area plaster are one continuous polygon at the same 2.75 m finished level, without a false seam at the living-room opening.
- Master: concealed window strip and two modest low-glare downlights toward the entry/foot side, avoiding the bed pillow area.
- B2: concealed window strip and two recessed deep-cup anti-glare downlights with approximately 60° total beam. One is on the desk-side aisle and aimed away from the monitor; one serves the wardrobe. A 60° beam alone does not prove comfort: check actual source shielding, diffuser, seated/standing sightlines, dimming and monitor reflections with a working sample before cutting plaster. A desk task lamp remains appropriate. The niche circulator stays. B3 remains Scheme A.

## Yard service layout

- The developer floor plan shows the washing machine at the Bath 2/service end of the yard, and the actual-unit photo shows the water tap and floor trap there. The previous viewer had them reversed. The model now puts the washer/dryer at that service end and the two litter boxes at the opposite dry end. The electrical drawing's three yard socket markers remain provisional: check the actual outlet serving the washer/dryer, waste route, floor-trap access and AC-ledge-door clearance before buying a stack or fixing storage.

## Scheme C — recommended hybrid for daily living

- The common area and hall alone receive the continuous 100 mm flat ceiling, finished at approximately 2.75 m. The living fan remains anchored to the slab. Dining and wet-kitchen lighting follows Scheme B's furniture and worktop positions, with separate circuits; no fixture sits over the sofa.
- The living window edge has the concealed continuous LED slot from Scheme B. The Scheme A sofa-wall box and globe beside the fan are omitted. The three pendants above the 1370 mm prep counter are retained as requested, but with restrained approximately 120 mm diameter shades and a higher bottom edge, so they read lighter beside the dining pendant.
- Master retains Scheme A's original 2.85 m ceiling, full-wall double curtains and window pelmet, plus separate shielded bedside lamps and the existing central surface light. B2 retains the original ceiling, broad central fixture and wardrobe niche; an adjustable plug-in desk lamp serves monitor work without adding two overhead downlights. B3 retains Scheme A unchanged.
- Scheme C is a third visual option, not a new measured electrical drawing. Confirm the fan fixing, relocated common-area cable routes, pendant clearance, driver access and actual switched circuits before issuing contractor instructions.

## Construction checks before ordering

Confirm the true slab height and measured room edges, permissible 100 mm cavity and fixture/driver depth, local fire/management restrictions, fan structural anchors and minimum blade height, switch circuits and cable routing, cabinet door and AC clearances, and the exact pendant suspension height. These checks cannot be proved by the viewer or a screenshot. The viewer deliberately does not claim photometric lux or glare accuracy.

The linked `dist/contractor.html` is a printable on-site measurement and pricing brief for Scheme C. It separates measured source/owner facts, model design targets and the blank measurements that must be filled by the contractor. Its socket register includes the owner's three dining and three yard outlet groups; horizontal offsets are intentionally blank because neither the developer electrical drawing nor the 3D markers supplies construction-grade distances.
